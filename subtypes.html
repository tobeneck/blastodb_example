Subtype 1 (ST1)

Commonly reported in humans; also detected across a range of mammals. FASTA: 5 sequences.

ST1 is one of the frequently detected Blastocystis subtypes in human studies. It has also been reported from non-human hosts, which means zoonotic transmission is plausible in some settings.

Subtype 2 (ST2)

Reported in humans; also detected in other mammals in multiple surveys. FASTA: 5 sequences.

ST2 is commonly included among the subtypes detected in humans. Like ST1, it has been reported from non-human hosts, so host-to-host transmission may vary by environment and contact patterns.

Subtype 3 (ST3)

Often among the most frequently detected subtypes in human cohorts. FASTA: 4 sequences.

ST3 is frequently reported in human studies and is sometimes described as relatively “human-associated” compared to some other subtypes, although host distributions vary across regions and sampling.

Subtype 4 (ST4)

Detected in humans and non-human hosts; prevalence can be region-dependent. FASTA: 4 sequences.

ST4 is reported in humans in multiple studies and is also detected in non-human hosts. Geographic and host patterns can differ substantially across datasets.

Subtype 5 (ST5)

Often associated with livestock (commonly reported in pigs and other hoofed animals); occasionally reported in humans. FASTA: 6 sequences.

ST5 is frequently discussed as a subtype commonly detected in certain livestock hosts. Human detection is reported less often than for ST1–ST4 in many cohorts.

Subtype 7 (ST7)

Often associated with avian hosts; sometimes detected in humans. FASTA: 10 sequences.
